How it works

The app does the planning on your iPad or iPhone. The free Connector on your PC adds the live data from the sim.

  1. Get the GA-EFB app from the App Store and create your free GA-EFB account in it. Your 7-day trial starts the first time you use it.
  2. Install the free GA-EFB Connector on the PC you fly on. No administrator password, and it starts with Windows from then on.
  3. Pair them once: press Pair in the Connector and scan its code in the app. Whenever MSFS is running, the app shows your aircraft, follows your plog and logs your flights.
  4. Enjoy it? Subscribe in the app, yearly or monthly, through the App Store. It unlocks on all your devices signed in to your account.

Questions

Setting up or stuck? See the user guide. Anything else, or an idea? See Support or email support@ga-efb.co.uk.

What do I need?

An iPad or iPhone and an email address for your free GA-EFB account. For the live data from the sim, a Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit) with Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 running the free GA-EFB Connector, on the same Wi-Fi as your iPad or iPhone.

Do I need the PC to use it?

No. Planning, weather, NOTAMs, weight and balance, checklists, charts and your logbook all work on the iPad or iPhone by itself. The Connector adds what comes from the sim: your aircraft on the map, the plog and kneeboard following you, the flight tracker and landing reports.

Does it work with Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020?

GA-EFB is made and tested for MSFS 2024. Much of it doesn't need the sim at all, but the live features are only supported with MSFS 2024.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet: GA-EFB is an app for iPad and iPhone.

Do I need other subscriptions?

No. The optional airliner flight plans use SimBrief, with its own free account.

How many devices can I use it on?

All of your own iPads and iPhones. Your subscription is tied to your GA-EFB account, so sign in to it on each device.

What happens after the free trial?

After 7 days GA-EFB asks you to subscribe, yearly or monthly, in the app. Your logbook, plogs and settings from the trial are all kept.

How do I cancel?

In your Apple ID's subscriptions (Settings → your name → Subscriptions on the iPad or iPhone), or from GA-EFB's Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription. GA-EFB carries on until the end of the period you've paid for.

I bought GA-EFB for £20 before

Thank you! Your licence is tied to your GA-EFB account, so sign in to the app with that account and it's unlocked, with nothing more to pay.

Can I get a refund?

Subscriptions are sold by Apple, so refunds are Apple's: see the refund policy.

Can I use it for real flying?

No. GA-EFB is for flight simulation only. Nothing in it is approved or kept up to date for real aviation.
