GA-EFB Connector

Free, for the GA-EFB app on iPad and iPhone

The GA-EFB app plans, briefs and keeps your logbook on its own. The free GA-EFB Connector, on the PC you fly Microsoft Flight Simulator on, adds the live data from the sim: your aircraft on the map, the flight tracker logging your flights and landings, weight and fuel from the aircraft, and GSX and add-on controls.

Download for WindowsFree · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Setting it up

  1. Install it on the PC you fly on: open GA-EFB-Connector-Setup.exe. It doesn't need an administrator, and it starts by itself with Windows from then on.
  2. The first time, Windows may ask whether Node.js may use the network. Allow it on Private networks: that's how the app reaches it over your home Wi-Fi.
  3. Pair your iPad or iPhone: open GA-EFB Connector from the Start menu and press Pair an iPad or iPhone. In the GA-EFB app, go to Settings → Simulator → Scan pairing QR and scan the code within 90 seconds.

That's it. Whenever the Connector and MSFS are running and your device is on the same Wi-Fi, the app shows the live data.

Good to know

  • Private and secure. Everything between the app and the Connector is encrypted with a key only your device and your PC have, set up by scanning the code. Nothing goes through the internet.
  • Your flights go to your logbook. Flights and landings the Connector records are moved into the app's logbook, and so to your GA-EFB account, the next time the app is connected.
  • Not GA-EFB for the PC. The Connector is a separate program, with no planning screens of its own and no account. It doesn't change GA-EFB for the PC if you have that too.
  • Unpairing. The Connector's page lists your paired devices, with Unpair beside each. Uninstall it from Windows Settings → Apps like any program.

For flight simulation only — not for real-world navigation or flight planning.
