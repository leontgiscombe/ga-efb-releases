Support

Need a hand with GA-EFB, found something that isn't right, or got an idea to make it better? You're in the right place.

Get in touch

Email support@ga-efb.co.uk. We read every message and usually reply within two working days.

To help us sort a problem quickly, please include:

  • your iPad or iPhone model, and its iOS version;
  • GA-EFB's version (Settings → About);
  • if it's about the sim: your Windows version and the GA-EFB Connector's version;
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead, with a screenshot if you can.

The quickest way is Settings → About → Send Feedback in the app: it starts an email with your device and the app's version already filled in.

Ideas and feedback

GA-EFB is made by a PPL student who flies it in the sim, and it gets better from what people who use it say. Tell us what you'd like it to do, what's missing for where you fly, or what's awkward to use, at support@ga-efb.co.uk.

Common questions

The user guide covers setting up, flying a VFR flight and every app. The questions we're asked most:

The app can't find the Connector
  • Check the iPad or iPhone and the PC are on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network or mobile data).
  • Check the Connector is running: open GA-EFB Connector from the Start menu.
  • The Windows firewall may be blocking it: see Problems and how to fix them in the guide.
  • Still not connecting? Unpair it in the Connector and in Settings → Simulator, then pair again.
It says the simulator is offline

The Connector connects to MSFS 2024 by itself once the sim is running. Give it a few seconds, and check you're in a flight rather than the main menu.

I subscribed but it hasn't unlocked

Go to Settings → Subscription and press Restore Purchases. If you subscribed while signed in to a different GA-EFB account, sign in with that one. Still locked? Email us.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In GA-EFB's Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription, or your Apple ID's subscriptions (Settings → your name → Subscriptions on the iPad or iPhone). GA-EFB carries on until the end of the period you've paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Subscriptions are sold by Apple, so ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. For a £20 licence bought before, email us within 14 days. See the refund policy.

I bought GA-EFB for £20 before

Your licence is tied to your GA-EFB account: sign in to the app with that account and it's unlocked, with nothing more to pay.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, Settings → your account → Delete Account. Or email us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it. See the privacy policy.

For flight simulation only GA-EFB is made for Microsoft Flight Simulator. We can't help with using it in a real aircraft: it isn't approved or maintained for real aviation, and must never be used to plan or fly one.
