Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

This explains what personal information GA-EFB uses, why, and your rights. GA-EFB is made by Leon Giscombe, a sole trader in England, who is responsible for your information (the "controller"). Contact: support@ga-efb.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Your GA-EFB account: your email address and password. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by our account provider. If you use Sign in with Apple instead, Apple gives us an email address (your own, or a private relay address that forwards to you if you choose Hide My Email) and an identifier for your Apple ID that only GA-EFB uses, and there's no password. We don't ask Apple for your name.
  • Your licence: when your free trial started, and whether your account has a subscription, or which licence key is tied to it.
  • What you keep in GA-EFB, so it's the same on all your devices: your logbook, landings, plogs, notes, aircraft profiles, settings and preferences, the name you enter in Settings (if you add one; it's only used to greet you in the app), and a profile picture if you add one. Any usernames and keys you enter for other services (such as SimBrief) are kept with them.
  • Your App Store subscription: Apple tells us which plan you have, its transaction number, its price, when it started and renews or ended, and whether it was refunded. We keep that with your account, so it's licensed on all your devices. The subscription doesn't tell us your Apple ID, name or card details.
  • Error reports from the app: when one of the app's pages goes wrong, the app sends us what went wrong (the error's message and where in GA-EFB's code it happened), which page you were on, which version of GA-EFB, and whether it's an iPad or iPhone and its iOS version. Reports don't include your account, email address or anything that identifies you or your device, and we keep them for 30 days.
  • Emails you send us, if you get in touch.

GA-EFB doesn't show adverts, doesn't track you for advertising and doesn't sell your information. This website doesn't use cookies or analytics.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, your licence and GA-EFB itself, including keeping your data the same on your devices. This is needed for our agreement with you (the "contract" legal basis).
  • To stop the free trial, subscriptions or licence keys being misused. This is our legitimate interest in selling GA-EFB fairly.
  • To keep sales records, which the law requires.
  • To find and fix faults in GA-EFB, from the app's error reports. This is our legitimate interest in making GA-EFB work properly.
  • To answer your emails.
  • To email you about your account (confirming your email address, resetting your password), and now and then about GA-EFB itself, for example when it arrives on the App Store. You can stop the news emails at any time with the unsubscribe link in each one; account emails still come when you ask for them. This is our legitimate interest in telling our own users about GA-EFB.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores GA-EFB accounts, licences and the data you keep in GA-EFB.
  • Apple sells GA-EFB's subscriptions and handles their payments, tax, receipts and refunds. Its own privacy policy covers your purchase.
  • Cloudflare runs a small service of ours that asks Apple about your subscription (with its transaction number and your GA-EFB account's id), that passes the app's rain radar and openAIP requests on, and that receives the app's error reports. When you delete an account made with Sign in with Apple, it tells Apple to end GA-EFB's use of your Apple ID.
  • Resend sends GA-EFB's emails (account emails and news) from ga-efb.co.uk, and tells us whether they were delivered.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the GA-EFB Connector's downloads, and sees your IP address when you use them.

Some of these companies may handle information outside the UK. Where they do, they protect it as UK data protection law requires, for example with the UK's approved contract terms.

The GA-EFB app runs on your iPad or iPhone. To show you weather, maps, airspace and other information, it contacts those services directly from your device (for example aviationweather.gov, MET Norway, NOAA, EUMETSAT, CARTO, Esri, OurAirports, the X-Plane Scenery Gateway, GeoNames, NATS and SimBrief, depending on the features you use; rain radar from Rainbow Weather and openAIP data come through our Cloudflare service). The GA-EFB Connector talks only to your own devices on your home network. Those services receive your IP address, and anything you choose to use with them (such as your SimBrief username), under their own privacy policies. If you add your own OpenWeatherMap key (Settings → Weather & Limits) for the optional cloud layer, the app sends that key and the part of the map you're looking at straight to OpenWeatherMap; the key is kept with your GA-EFB settings so it works on all your devices.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and data while you have a GA-EFB account. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and your data, apart from records we must keep by law, such as sales records, which are kept for six years. To stop the free trial being used twice, we keep a one-way scrambled fingerprint of your email address, from which the address can't be read back.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your information, and object to or limit how we use it. Email support@ga-efb.co.uk and we'll reply within a month. GA-EFB's Back Up & Restore also lets you save a copy of your data at any time.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, though we'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Children

GA-EFB isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in GA-EFB about any important change.
