Refund policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

GA-EFB has a 7-day free trial, so you can try everything before you subscribe. We still want you to be happy with it.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are bought in the GA-EFB app from Apple, so refunds are Apple's to give: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with the Apple ID you subscribed with. Apple's refunds go back to the way you paid.

Cancelling

Cancel any time in your Apple ID's subscriptions (Settings → your name → Subscriptions on the iPad or iPhone), or from GA-EFB's Settings → Subscription → Manage Subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; GA-EFB carries on until the end of the period you've paid for.

Licences bought before

If you bought a one-off GA-EFB licence through Stripe, you can have a full refund within 14 days of buying it, for any reason: email us at support@ga-efb.co.uk with the email address you bought with. It goes back to the way you paid, usually within 5 to 10 working days depending on your bank.

After a refund

When a subscription or licence is refunded, GA-EFB goes back to asking you to subscribe, within a day. Your GA-EFB account and your data (logbook, plogs, notes and settings) are kept unless you ask us to delete them.

Something not working?

If GA-EFB doesn't work as described, please get in touch and we'll help. Your legal rights as a consumer aren't affected by this policy.
